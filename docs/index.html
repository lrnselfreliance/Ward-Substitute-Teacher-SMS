Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder

A text-message service for arranging substitute teachers.

What it does

When a teacher needs a substitute for a Sunday class, this service texts available volunteers to ask whether they can cover that date. When someone accepts, the teacher and the substitute are sent each other's contact details so they can coordinate directly.

How to join

  1. Text the Service's phone number, available from ward leadership.
  2. Reply YES when asked whether you consent to receive texts.
  3. Answer a few short questions: your name, whether you teach or substitute, and which Sundays you are available.

Participation is voluntary and free. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP at any time for a list of commands, or STOP to be removed.
