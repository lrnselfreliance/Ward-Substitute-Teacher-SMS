Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder

A free text-message service for arranging substitute teachers for Sunday classes.

Who operates this service

Operated by Clover Leaf Ward Primary, a volunteer, for members of Clover Leaf Ward, a local congregation. Contact: substitute@learningselfreliance.com

What the service does

Volunteer teachers occasionally need someone to cover their Sunday class. This service keeps a list of members willing to substitute, texts them when a class needs covering, and puts the teacher and the substitute in touch with each other so they can coordinate directly.

It is free and non-commercial. It sends no advertising, marketing, or promotional messages of any kind.

Messaging program

  • Who receives messages: only members of Clover Leaf Ward who signed themselves up by texting the service first.
  • What is sent: requests asking whether you can substitute on a specific Sunday, and a confirmation with the teacher's name and phone number if you accept.
  • How often: message frequency varies, and depends on how often substitutes are needed. Typically a few messages a month.
  • Quiet hours: no messages are sent between 9pm and 8am.
