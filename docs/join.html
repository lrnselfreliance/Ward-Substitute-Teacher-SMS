Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder

A free text-message service for arranging substitute teachers for Sunday classes. Operated by Clover Leaf Ward Primary, a volunteer, for members of Clover Leaf Ward. Contact: substitute@learningselfreliance.com

What this service does

Volunteer teachers at Clover Leaf Ward occasionally need someone to cover their Sunday class. This service keeps a list of members willing to substitute, texts them when a class needs covering, and puts the teacher and the substitute in touch with each other. It is free, non-commercial, and sends no advertising of any kind.

Who receives messages: only members of Clover Leaf Ward who have signed up themselves using the instructions below.

How to sign up

Text JOIN to

(385) 354-7328

By texting JOIN to this number you agree to receive recurring text messages from the Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder about substitute teaching. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Reply HELP for help, reply STOP to opt out. See our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

What happens next

  1. You will receive one text asking whether you consent to receive messages about substitute teaching.
  2. Reply YES to join, or NO and you will not be contacted again.
  3. If you join, a few short questions follow: your name, whether you teach or substitute, and which Sundays you are available.

What you will receive

  • Occasional texts asking whether you can substitute on a specific Sunday
  • A confirmation, with the teacher's name and phone number, if you accept

Nothing else. No advertising, no announcements, no promotional messages. You are never contacted between 9pm and 8am.

Leaving the list

Reply STOP at any time to be removed immediately. Reply PAUSE to stop being asked temporarily without leaving, and RESUME to start again. Reply HELP for a list of commands.

Participation is voluntary and free. Your name and phone number are shared only with the teacher whose class you agree to cover. See the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
