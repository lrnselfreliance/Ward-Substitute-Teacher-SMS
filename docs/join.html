Need a substitute? Willing to be one?

Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder — a free text service for arranging substitute teachers for Sunday classes.

Text JOIN to

TEXT_NUMBER

By texting this number you agree to receive text messages about substitute teaching. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

What happens next

  1. You will receive one text asking whether you consent to receive messages about substitute teaching.
  2. Reply YES to join, or NO and you will not be contacted again.
  3. If you join, a few short questions follow: your name, whether you teach or substitute, and which Sundays you are available.

What you will receive

  • Occasional texts asking whether you can substitute on a specific Sunday
  • A confirmation, with the teacher's name and phone number, if you accept

Nothing else. No advertising, no announcements, no promotional messages. You are never contacted between 9pm and 8am.

Leaving the list

Reply STOP at any time to be removed immediately. Reply PAUSE to stop being asked temporarily without leaving, and RESUME to start again. Reply HELP for a list of commands.

Participation is voluntary and free. Your name and phone number are shared only with the teacher whose class you agree to cover. See the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.
