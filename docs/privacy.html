Privacy Policy

Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder · Effective August 16, 2026

The Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder ("the Service") is a text-message service that helps members of Clover Leaf Ward arrange substitute teachers for Sunday classes. This policy explains what information the Service collects, how it is used, and how to stop using it.

Who operates the Service

The Service is operated by Clover Leaf Ward for the sole purpose of coordinating substitute teachers within the congregation. It is not a commercial service and sends no advertising.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to match substitutes to classes:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your name, as you provide it
  • Whether you serve as a teacher, a substitute, or both
  • Your gender, used only to match substitutes to classes of the same gender
  • The class you teach, if you are a teacher
  • Which Sundays of the month you are available
  • A record of your consent, including its date and time
  • A record of substitute requests, who was asked, and who accepted

We do not collect payment information, location data, contacts, or any information beyond what is listed above.

How we use your information

Your information is used only to:

  • Ask whether you are available to substitute on a specific date
  • Share your name and phone number with the specific teacher whose class you have agreed to cover, so the two of you can coordinate directly
  • Keep the rotation fair by tracking when you last substituted
  • Let ward leaders see whether classes have been covered

How we share your information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Within the Service, your name and phone number are shared only with the teacher whose class you have agreed to substitute for, and with ward leaders administering the Service. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.

Text messages are delivered through Twilio, our messaging provider, which processes messages on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy commitments.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies and depends on how often substitutes are needed. In a typical month a participant receives only a few messages. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

How to stop receiving messages

Reply STOP to any message to be removed immediately and permanently. You will receive one confirmation and nothing further. You may also reply PAUSE to temporarily stop being asked without leaving the list, and RESUME to start again. Reply HELP at any time for assistance.

Data retention and security

Records are kept while you participate, and for a reasonable period afterwards so that the substitute rotation remains fair. Information is stored on a private system accessible only to those administering the Service. If you opt out, we retain your phone number solely to honor your opt-out request.

Children

The Service is intended for adults who teach or substitute for classes. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, participants will be notified by text message before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the Clover Leaf Ward Primary at substitute@learningselfreliance.com.
