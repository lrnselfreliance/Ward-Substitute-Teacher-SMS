Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder · Effective August 16, 2026

Program description

The Clover Leaf Ward Substitute Finder is a text-message service that helps members of Clover Leaf Ward arrange substitute teachers for Sunday classes. When a teacher needs a substitute, the Service texts available volunteers to ask whether they can cover that date. When someone accepts, the Service sends the teacher and the substitute each other's name and phone number so they can coordinate directly.

The Service is provided free of charge by Clover Leaf Ward for the benefit of its congregation. It is not a commercial service and sends no advertising or promotional content.

How to join

Participation is entirely voluntary. You join by sending a text message to the Service's phone number. The Service will reply asking whether you consent to receive text messages. Only after you reply YES will the Service ask for any information or send you any further messages. Replying NO ends the conversation.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies and depends on how often substitutes are needed. In a typical month a participant receives only a few messages.

Message and data rates may apply. The Service itself is free, but your mobile carrier may charge you for sending and receiving text messages according to your plan. Check with your carrier if you are unsure.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message from the Service. You will be removed immediately and will receive a single confirmation message and nothing further. You may rejoin later by texting the number again.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for a list of commands, or contact the Clover Leaf Ward Primary at substitute@learningselfreliance.com.

Supported carriers

The Service works with major United States mobile carriers, including AT&T, Verizon Wireless, T-Mobile, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Delivery is not guaranteed

Text message delivery depends on your mobile carrier and network conditions outside our control. The Service may fail to find a substitute, and messages may be delayed or not delivered at all. Participants should not rely on the Service as the only means of arranging coverage for a class.

Your responsibilities

If you agree to substitute for a class, you are expected to attend or to contact the teacher directly if your plans change. Please keep your availability current by texting SUNDAYS followed by the Sundays you can serve.

Privacy

Information collected by the Service is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. Your name and phone number are shared only with the teacher whose class you have agreed to cover, and with ward leaders administering the Service.

Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be announced by text message before they take effect.
